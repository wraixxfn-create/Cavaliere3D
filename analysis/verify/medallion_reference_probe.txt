--- what that means at the waist
    checkpoint-10 mount: radius 43 mm, face at Y 0.1430, belt band Z 1.005-1.105 (belt height 100 mm)
    reference motif:     core 27 mm, bezel 30-43 mm radius (peak 36 mm), rays to 86 mm radius,
                         about 21 gold spokes

    The ray reach is the one number that does NOT transfer.  Taken literally the
    86 mm ray tips are a 171 mm emblem on a 100 mm belt band, and the gold tests
    that produced that figure are reading the tabard's embroidery and chainwork
    as much as the motif's own points.  What transfers is the composition: a thin
    gold ring around a near-black centre, with many short blades crowding the
    outside of the ring and reaching roughly one blade-length past it.

    checkpoint 11 (delivered, built by tools/build_medallion.py):
       ring        59.3 mm across (29.7 mm rim radius), wall 7.3 mm - the low end
                   of the reference's 30-43 mm bezel radius, held there so the
                   whole ornament stays inside the 43 mm mount radius and the
                   plain bezel of checkpoint 8 keeps showing around the gold
       centre      48.2 mm outer diameter, dark (MAT_Orn_ShadowStone_01), set
                   2.31 mm behind the ring's crest, 2.8 mm in front of the belt
       blades      16 blades in two alternating scales, primaries 15.1-16.5 mm of
                   run and minors 11.6-13.3 mm, tips to 40.2 mm radius
       overall     76.8 mm across = 20.9 % of the 367.9 mm belt width

    Note on scale: this probe uses its own 1.9049 mm/px fit to ref_front.png, while
    the crown work (tools/crown_reference_probe.py) uses Prompt 4's 1.8323 mm/px
    chin calibration.  The two agree on the ring to within 4 %, and both are
    quoted in README.md rather than averaged away.

    The rear ornament is the same design at 88 %, seated on the lower back plate
    (checkpoint 11), because the reference carries the motif on the back as well -
    the back motif's own gold test is much weaker (0.16 vs 0.86 on the ring),
    which is what the darker, smaller back piece matches.
